# Draft wording: offers from businesses (for client approval)

**Status:** draft for Shaheb Bazar to approve by Tuesday 27 October 2026 (KAN-284). Not yet on the site.
Once approved, it is added to the Privacy Policy (`/privacy`) and Terms of Service (`/terms`) and
the "updated" dates change.

---

## Privacy Policy: new section "Offers from businesses"

> **Offers from businesses**
>
> If you save a business, we may ask whether you want to receive offers from it by SMS or email.
> This is your choice: nothing is ticked for you, and saving a business does not sign you up.
>
> If you agree, that business can send you offers through Shahebbazar. We send them for the
> business, so the business never receives your phone number or email address. Each choice
> applies to one business only.
>
> You can stop at any time:
>
> - with the unsubscribe link in every offer, which works without signing in
> - under each business on **My account → Saved businesses**
> - by removing the business from your saved businesses
>
> Stopping takes effect immediately. We keep a record of when you agreed and when you stopped,
> so we can show that offers were only sent with your permission.

## Privacy Policy: change to section 4 "What we never do"

Add one line:

> - we do not send you offers from a business unless you have chosen to receive them

## Terms of Service: new section for businesses "Sending offers"

> **Sending offers to customers**
>
> Businesses with a Marketing pass can send offers through Shahebbazar to customers who have
> chosen to receive offers from that business. You agree that:
>
> - offers go only through Shahebbazar, and only to customers who have chosen to receive them
> - you will not upload, import or collect customer contact details from the site to message
>   people outside it
> - offers must be accurate: real prices, real stock and real terms
> - every offer includes the unsubscribe link Shahebbazar adds, and you will not try to remove or
>   hide it
>
> Shahebbazar may suspend a business's Marketing pass if these terms are broken.

---

### Notes for the client

- The wording assumes offers are sent by Shahebbazar on the business's behalf, as agreed on
  20 October (sender shown as "\<Business\> via Shaheb Bazar"), so businesses never see
  customers' contact details.
- Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 is expected to apply from about May 2027.
  This wording is written to fit a consent-based approach in the meantime; a local legal review
  before launch is recommended.
- A Bangla version of these sections will be prepared once the English wording is approved.
